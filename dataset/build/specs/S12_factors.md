# S12 factor shares, labour groups and factor taxes

Built 2026-10-23. The stage entry is `dataset/build/s12_factors.py`; the method is the package `dataset/build/s12_factors/` (`method.py` the I-O split, ch.14A SUR, ch.14B wage calibration and ch.15 taxes; `labour.py` the ILOSTAT country shares; `sources.py` readers; `checks.py` hard checks; `config.py` strict config). The screens are `dataset/build/screens/s12_suite.py`; the mutation tests are `dataset/build/tests/test_s12_checks.py`. Choices are `dataset/build/config/s12/s12_choices.csv`; screen explanations are `config/s12/screen_explanations.csv`. A choice row that is missing, duplicated, unknown, off its implemented value or without `decided_by` and `decided_at` stops the run (`S12-Hconfig`).

The method is GTAP 12 ch.14A (agricultural factor shares), ch.14B (labour by occupation) and ch.15 (income and factor taxes), with the labour/capital split of the I-O tables (Aguiar et al. 2025 p.8) and the ch.9D donor rule. David's rule for this version: follow GTAP, and log possible improvements in `docs/IMPROVEMENTS_LOG.md` (S12 section) rather than apply them.

## Inputs

Upstream stamps S01 (regions, sectors, ISIC concordance, ISCO groups), S02 (GDP, population), S05 (composite donors and weights), S10 (income and factor tax revenue, domestic support) and S11 (`io_s11` and the S11 energy output values). Data: Eurostat FIGARO 2022 value-added rows (`data/figaro_va/`), OECD National Accounts Table 6 (`data/oecd_na_table6/`), ILOSTAT employment and earnings (`data/ilostat/`), USDA ERS Fuglie cost shares (`data/usda_ers/`), WDI natural-resource rents 2021 (`data/wdi_rents_2021/`), WDI arable land and GDP per capita (`data/wdi_ag_2022/`), PWT 11.0 labsh (report only).

## Steps, in the GTAP order

1. **Labour and capital in value added.** For each ICIO economy, compensation of employees (D1) over D1 + gross operating surplus and mixed income (B2A3G) by industry, from FIGARO 2022 (49 economies), else OECD Table 6 at the latest year at or before 2022 with both items (B2A3G = B1G − D1 − D29X39 where Table 6 omits it, CHL). A source missing at most five sectors is used and the missing sectors filled as below (COL, NZL, CHL). A sector takes the codes inside it when they cover it exactly (pap = C17 + C18, trd = G45 + G46 + G47), else the smallest code that contains it. Other net production taxes (D29X39) come out of VA by industry (fix round A3, below). Where FIGARO has only a parent code (section B), the parent is split by the economy's own Table 6 division values (USA, CAN, MEX, SAU, NLD mining); a FIGARO section whose codes all carry one imputed share (IND manufacturing) keeps its D1 total and takes the pattern of the nearest economy that observes the codes (A5, Grok-F2). Shares outside [0, 1] are clipped and flagged. A sector with neither D1 nor B2A3G in the source (FIGARO T, households as employers, for AUS, BGR, CHN, IND, JPN, ROU and ZAF; Costa Rica mining) and an extraction cell with no surplus are not observed and take the share of the nearest economy by S02 GDP per capita in the same GTAP15 pool that observes the sector (`unobserved_sector`, `extraction_no_surplus`). An ICIO economy with no split takes its ch.9D donor (nearest GDP per capita in its pool among economies with a split; the world where the pool has none: EGY, MAR, TUN). Non-ICIO regions combine their S05 donors, weighted by member weight times donor sector VA. Negative VA cells (three, air) go whole to capital and are flagged for S13. A composite member whose B06 column S05 builds from an oil-producer donor (`oil_donor` in donor_weights) takes that donor's oil and gas split (B8 follow-up).
1b. **Self-employed and capital floor (fix round A5, A6).** Labour = D1 x (1 + SLF / EES) by ISIC section (Gollin 2002, adjustment 2; ILOSTAT EMP_TEMP_SEX_STE_ECO, latest year at or before 2022, else the nearest economy by GDP per capita in the pool), capped so that capital keeps its floor: CFC / (D1 + B2A3G) from Table 6 P51C, else the GTAP15 pool median, else the world median. hhe stays all labour and crp keeps the ch.14A shares. Hard check `S12-Hfloor`. Default since the next round (David 2026-10-25, `labour_definition`; `mixed_income` must match, or the config stops): GTAP 12 takes labour from each contributor's table, and contributors impute the self-employed this way (ch.8A §A.4 item 6, eq. 8.1, AdjustedCOE = OriginalCOE × TW/NOE; ch.8Q §Q.4 item 4); applying it to every region is our choice. The alternative `compensation_of_employees_d1` stays in code.
1c. **Capital-income floor on VDEP (`capital_vdep_floor`, our choice, not in the GTAP text).** The model needs capital income above depreciation, and VDEP is 4% of VKB (GTAP 12 ch.7.3, S02), not tied to the factor split. After the natural-resource step, where a region's capital income after the ch.15 taxes (ΣEVOS capital) is below 1.25 × VDEP, the smallest share b of its Gollin addition (labour × (1 − D1 share / Gollin share), cell by cell, under the same capital-floor cap) that meets 1.25 × VDEP goes back to capital, one b for the region (b = 1 is labour at D1). Cell VA, land, natural resources, the output tax and the regional tax totals do not move. The 1.25 margin covers the S13 FIT move on capital income seen in these regions; S14-Hsi stays the hard gate on the final data. On the 2026-10-25 data it binds in GNQ (b = 0.271) and TLS (b = 0.125) only. Hard check `S12-Hvdepk`, flag CAPITAL-VDEP-FLOOR, one ledger row per region, `output/vdep_floor_s12.csv`. `va_split_s12.csv` keeps the Gollin split before the floor in `labour_musd` and the final split in `labour_after_natres_musd` and `capital_after_natres_musd`.
1d. **Gollin capped at the ILO labour income share (`gollin_ilo_cap`, beta v4 G2/P40, default off, our choice, not in the GTAP text).** When on, before the VDEP floor, a fraction b of each region's Gollin addition (one b per region, pro rata over the cells, b ≤ 1 so labour never falls below D1) goes back to capital so that the region's labour income is at most the ILO SDG 10.4.1 labour income share (ILOSTAT `LAP_2GDP_NOC_RT`, latest year at or before the base year, GDP-weighted over the members with a value, used only when those members hold at least half the region's S02 GDP) times S02 GDP. A region below the ILO share is not raised. Report check `S12-Rilocap`, flag LAB-ILO-CAP, one ledger row per capped region, `output/ilo_cap_s12.csv`.

## Running

`cd dataset/build && python3 s12_factors.py` (about 30 seconds). Float outputs are rounded to 1e-6. `GTAP_STAGES_DIR=<dir>` runs it against a scratch copy of the upstream stages; `S12_PREV_OUTPUT=<dir>` turns on screen 7. Tests: `python3 -m unittest tests.test_s12_checks`.

## Round-3 final QA2 item 7: land income tax cap

Choice `land_income_tax_cap` (`gtap`: ch.17B p.3, no sign violation): `method.cap_land_income_tax` caps every land cell's income tax at 99% of its land rent less its positive factor-use tax (the S13 `EVOS_FLOOR_SHARE` floor); the excess is dropped and ledgered (`LAND-ITAX-CAPPED`), `S12-Htax` nets it (`land_itax_dropped_musd` in `factor_tax_s12.csv`) and `S12-Hlanditax` (hard) checks the cap. In the S12 tables the cap does not bind for TLS and TTO; their S13 caps come from land rent shrinking in the fit.
