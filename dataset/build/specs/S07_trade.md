# S07 merchandise trade (GTAP trade module, goods)

Spec for the 2022 build, S07R. Precedence: this file, then `PLAN.md` §3(c) as amended by erratum E45, then the GTAP texts cited below. Decision 1 was reversed by David on 2026-10-21 13:52 UTC+7.

The stage reads the committed S01, S02, S04 and S06 snapshots, and the S09 services comparison for the re-export residual report. It does not rerun them. It does not read ITIC, BaTIS, EI or IEA. GTAP and JGEA texts are not inputs of the run and are not committed. The HS17 concordance is read from the S01 output path and is not committed (it carries `gtap_gsec3`).

`config/s07/trade_choices.csv` has one row per choice. The stage does not choose. `goods_source=baci_all_pairs` is built. `pri_vir_trade=in_baci_842` retires the Puerto Rico carve. The Census extract stays report-only.

## What GTAP says

Aguiar et al. 2025, JGEA 10(2), p.5 §2: "For exports and imports, GTAP relies on reconciled bilateral trade data for merchandise and services."

Aguiar et al. 2022, JGEA 7(2), p.6: "We use reconciled bilateral trade data for merchandise and services in the GTAP Data Base because the initial trade data is not balanced, i.e., world exports differ from world imports."

Aguiar et al. 2025 p.12 §3.2.6: "One of the objectives of the trade data reconciliation process is to ensure that re-exports are reassigned to the country of origin. Therefor, only domestically-produced exports are recorded." The same page: "the main objective is to produce balanced trade, i.e., world exports line up with world imports for each commodity." And: "Trade data for more than 50 countries with re-exports are estimated by deriving domestic exports and by converting total imports into retained imports."

Aguiar et al. 2022 p.11 §3.2.6: "One of the objectives of the reconciliation is to ensure that there are no re-exports in GTAP." And: "We use a concordance between the HS6 and GTAP sectors to aggregate the HS6 flows."

GTAP 7 ch.9A (Gehlhar, Wang and Yao 2010) p.1: "Indirect trade is not permitted in the GTAP Data Base and it must be dealt with." The same page treats the Netherlands and Singapore separately, and Hong Kong, China by an optimization model.

GTAP 7 ch.9A p.6 §9.A.4: "the actual trade flow is more likely represented by one of the country's reports rather than by an adjusted average of the both reports." The steps named there are: convert CIF import flows to FOB, separate non-reporter flows from mirror flows, and calculate reliability indicators. "The cif value is generated by reapplying the transport margins to the reconciled flows prior to aggregating to the GTAP sector level."

GTAP 7 ch.9A p.3: accuracy means discrepancies "no larger than 20 percent."

GTAP 12 ch.17B §B.9 p.7: "there is a trade module where merchandise and services trade data is prepared (Chapter 10)." The same section: the FIT module reconciles each region's I-O table with the trade, protection and energy data.

GTAP 12 ch.17B §B.2 p.3: the interim file is merged with the bilateral trade data, and "the value of trade at basic prices are calculated from the protection revenue data (for imports and exports) and trade data at CIF prices."

GTAP 12 ch.17A §A.1 p.1: "bilateral trade flows from the COMTRADE database." p.2: FIT holds export and import vectors at border prices and import vectors at basic prices, and export and output tax rates.

GTAP 12 ch.9A p.3: "Since the trade data are central to the whole data reconciliation process, in these cases, it is the production targets, not the export targets, that must give way."

GTAP 12 ch.02 p.17 names VXSB (non-margin exports at basic prices), VFOB (FOB) and VCIF (imports at CIF). p.5: "Export Duties VFOB / VXSB."

Aguiar et al. 2022 p.9 and Aguiar et al. 2025 p.9: energy trade is bilateralized from IEA with UN Comtrade, because of "the widely-recognized weakness of UN-COMTRADE in capturing energy trade." Aguiar et al. 2025 p.14 and ch.10B p.2: electricity, gas distribution, water and dwellings are not built from services trade; energy trade uses IEA. ch.10B p.3: travelers' expenditures are allocated from private consumption.

GTAP 12 ch.10A (merchandise trade) has no public attachment. The resource-page abstract says Liechtenstein is reported within Switzerland. GTAP 7 ch.9A is the documented merchandise method. Aguiar et al. 2025 p.12 says GTAP 12 uses a new HS6 methodology (Gehlhar, 2026), which is not public.

Not in GTAP text, and not filled here: how a database that still has a rest-of-world block splits that block (GTAP has no ROW); whether trade between members of one composite sits on the diagonal; whether the exporter's domestic trade and transport margins are inside VXSB; how a mining aggregate is split into oil and gas beyond the HS concordance (Aguiar et al. 2022 p.11).

## What this stage does

David, 2026-10-21 13:52 UTC+7: goods are reconciled BACI for every pair (GTAP standard). ICIO pair values are a comparison file only. The moved-out HS goods are booked as merchandise. Timmy, 2026-10-21: the Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands carve is retired, because BACI code 842 already includes them. E37 is met by BACI. Census stays report-only.

The sixteen rows in `trade_choices.csv` are the rules. In short:

1. Re-export cleaning. The prep at `1fcc7ab` (`dataset/build/prep_trade/`) is run on BACI HS17 V202601, year 2022, hubs HKG then NLD then SGP. The stage uses `apply_hub_slim`, the same arithmetic without the HS6 ledger. BEL, ARE and PAN are not adjusted. H7 is kept.
2. Concordance. Cleaned HS6 flows use S01 `concord_hs17_to_sector.csv` (one sector, share 1). Countries use S01 `country_region_map.csv`. BACI 490 (ISO3 S19) is Taiwan. Flows are aggregated to the 167 regions before the hubs, so the USA cell is BACI code 842 and the CAN cell is code 124. ISL stays inside XWE. There is no ISL split.
3. The 32 goods sectors are the 27 goods plus `wat`, `pub`, `art`, `pro` and `pst`. HS 271600 stays out and is flagged `ELY-TO-S11`. Three other concordance lines that S01 labels `ely` are remapped inside this stage, not in the concordance file: 220190 to `fbt`, 270500 to `gas`, 284450 to `chm`. Flag `ELY-HS-MOVED`. 270500 is GTAP GDT, which S01 merges into `ely`.
4. VXSB for every region pair is cleaned BACI FOB in USD million (thousand USD divided by 1,000). A diagonal `VXSB(r, r)` is kept only when `r` is a composite region (`source` `intra_composite`). A single-country diagonal is dropped and written to `reimport_dropped_s07r.csv` (flag `REIMPORT-DROPPED`). `vfob_musd` equals `vxsb_musd`. `pre_tax` is `yes`, so S10 can divide by the export-tax power. Off-diagonal `source` is `baci_all_pairs`. `basis` is `fob`. The file is `vxsb_goods_s07r.csv.gz`.
5. Oil and gas are the HS lines themselves: oil is 270900 and 271410, gas is 271111 and 271121, plus the remapped 270500 on gas. There is no ICIO B06 fallback.
6. The comparison file `icio_goods_compare_s07r.csv.gz` is built from `io_s04` with the previous sector rule (B06 kept, the five moved sectors included) and ICIO-to-ROW totals. ISL is labeled XWE. It is never used to set a value.
7. The re-export residual is like-for-like. BACI is the 27 goods industries (not `wat pub art pro pst`). ICIO is the matching goods industries, with oil and gas as one B06. The margin is ICIO `trd` plus `rdt` minus BaTIS `built_musd`, on `icio_pair` and `row_export` only. The ranking is the 79 regions with `icio_measured=yes`. Other regions, including XWE and the ISL margin row, are in the same file with `group=no_icio` and are not in the top 20. The file is `reexport_residual_s07r.csv`.
8. No I-O cell changes. `io_s07.csv.gz` is a byte copy of `io_s06.csv.gz`.

Zero cells and cells below 1e-12 USD million are not written. A USD-million figure is written with up to 10 decimal places.
