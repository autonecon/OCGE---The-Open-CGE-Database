# Türkiye–Tunisia free trade agreement (official text)

WTO-circulated text of the Association Agreement establishing a free trade area between Turkey and Tunisia (signed 25 November 2004, in force 1 July 2005; WTO RTA ID 35, document series WT/REG203), fetched 2026-10-23 from the WTO RTA database (https://rtais.wto.org/UI/PublicShowMemberRTAIDCard.aspx?rtaid=35) and WTO Documents Online. ## Finance-law provisions (added 2026-10-23, second pass)

From the Ministry of Finance site (www.finances.gov.tn/fr/loi_finance), which republishes the JORT text:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `jort_lf2018_loi_2017-66_ar.pdf` | Loi n° 2017-66 of 18 December 2017, finance law 2018, Arabic (JORT of 19 December 2017). **Art. 40** (p. 4278): products of Turkish origin in tariff chapters 33, 34, 40, 48, 61, 62, 63, 64, 72, 76, 84 and 85 that are on List II of the Tunisia–Türkiye FTA pay customs duty up to **90% of the general-regime (MFN) rate**, subject to the bound rates. The duty applies for two years from 1 January 2018. After that it is removed in three equal annual steps. |
| `jort_lf2020_loi_2019-78_fr.pdf` | Loi n° 2019-78 of 23 December 2019, finance law 2020, French (JORT n° 104 of 27 December 2019). **Art. 52** (p. 4437) amends art. 40: the duty now applies for **four years from 1 January 2018**, so through 2021. It is then removed over three years in equal annual steps. |

Reading for 2022, to be confirmed in S10: 2022 is the first year of the phase-out. With equal steps, the extra duty would be two thirds of 90% of MFN, that is about 60% of MFN, on the List II lines in those chapters. This assumes the first cut took effect on 1 January 2022. The finance laws for 2021 and 2022 have no later change (the Arabic texts were searched for "تركي").
