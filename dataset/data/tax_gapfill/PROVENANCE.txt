use: fills an income-tax wedge only where OECD Global Revenue Statistics category 1000 and the WDI central-government fallback both have no row. The figure is income-tax revenue as a percent of GDP, the same concept as category 1000. It is divided by the factor bill. A rate outside (0, 0.6) is not stored.
taiwan_2022: Ministry of Finance, Yearbook of Taxation, Republic of China 2024, Department of Statistics. Tax revenue was 14.2 percent of GDP in 2022. Income tax, individual plus profit-seeking enterprise, was 51.8 percent of tax revenue in 2022. 14.2 times 0.518 is 7.3556 percent of GDP.
taiwan_2017: the same yearbook gives 2017 income-tax collections of NT$986,412,046 thousand (individual NT$483,099,840 thousand, profit-seeking enterprise NT$503,312,206 thousand) but the tax-to-GDP sentence in that edition covers 2020-2022 only. No 2017 percent of GDP is stored.
qatar: Qatar has no personal income tax on wages. The income-tax concept in this database is OECD category 1000, which also includes corporate tax on profits. No Qatar corporate-tax revenue as a percent of GDP was in the national tables retrieved for this round, and IMF Article IV text is not copied into the repository. The stored rate stays zero. That zero is the missing revenue row, not a measured corporate-tax zero.
iran_algeria_labour: no revenue-to-GDP or employer-contribution row from a national statistical office was transcribed in this round. Those zeros stay missing-source zeros.
