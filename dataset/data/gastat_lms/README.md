# GASTAT (Saudi Arabia) Register-based Labour Market Statistics, Q4 2022

- **Folder:** `data/gastat_lms/`
- **Source page:** [https://www.stats.gov.sa/en/w/register-based-labour-market-statistics-q4-2022](https://www.stats.gov.sa/en/w/register-based-labour-market-statistics-q4-2022)
- **Download URL / API:** https://www.stats.gov.sa/documents/20117/2435259/LMS_2022_Q4_EN_1.xlsx_fixed_2466061/8a3dde4e-725b-ffa9-966b-9af4285a0405?t=1734052499723
- **Version:** Labour market 2022 Q4 (administrative registers)
- **Download date / origin:** 2026-10-20 via box browser; see FETCH_NOTES.md
- **Licence:** KSA Open Data Licence (Saudi national open data licence; use with attribution confirmed by David 2026-10-20)
- **Coverage (2022 or year-rule fallback):** Saudi Arabia, Q4 2022 (year rule: 2022). Social-insurance participants by main occupation group (tables 3-5 to 3-7) and by main economic activity group (tables 3-8 to 3-10); civil-service employees (4-x) with no occupation split; non-Saudi domestic workers by household occupation (7-2). NO occupation x industry cross table and NO wages.
- **Units:** persons
- **Valuation basis / concept:** administrative register counts, end of Q4 2022; excludes informal workers and some government staff
- **How it is used (PLAN.md stage):** Labour stage (GTAP 12 ch.14B): Saudi occupation totals and industry totals used as the RAS margins for the occupation x industry labour split (instead of a pure donor). Coverage gap (informal, part of government) to be flagged.
