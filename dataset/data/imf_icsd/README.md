- **Folder:** `data/imf_icsd/`
- **Source page:** [https://infrastructuregovern.imf.org/content/PIMA/Home/PimaTools/ICSD.html](https://infrastructuregovern.imf.org/content/PIMA/Home/PimaTools/ICSD.html)
- **Download URL / API:** https://infrastructuregovern.imf.org/content/dam/PIMA/Knowledge-Hub/dataset/IMFInvestmentandCapitalStockDataset2021.xlsx
- **Version:** 2021 release, data to 2019
- **Download date / origin:** 2026-10-20
- **Licence:** IMF terms of use (free with attribution)
- **Coverage (2022 or year-rule fallback):** 148 regions; latest 2019; QAT missing; LBY 2009
- **Units:** billions of constant 2017 national currency
- **Valuation basis / concept:** PIM capital stocks (general government, private, PPP)
- **How it is used (PLAN.md stage):** Stage (h): PRIMARY capital source per GTAP 12 ch.7 s7.3 (fix round 2, David 2026-10-20 22:02); ICSD K/GDP in its latest year, rolled forward to the base year (perpetual inventory, 4%). Zero kgov_n+kpriv_n (ABW, MAC, NRU, PRI, SOM, WBG) is treated as missing.
