# Stats SA 2022 domestic workers and their pay (beta v4 G4)

- **Folder:** `data/statssa_hhe_2022/`
- **Source:** Statistics South Africa publications. Stats SA's site blocks scripted downloads, so the files are Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) raw copies of the official URLs (in `data/MANIFEST.csv`). `.txt` files are pdftotext extracts.
- **Download date:** 2026-10-26 (by David's agent).
- **Licence:** Stats SA copyright; free reproduction with acknowledgement.
- **Year:** 2022.
- **Use:** S03 choice `household_employers_va` (beta v4 G4), through the transcribed row in `build/config/s03/household_employers_2022.csv`; not read by the build.
  - Labour Market Dynamics in South Africa 2022 (Report 02-11-02), Table 4.4: domestic workers 839 thousand in 2022 (txt line 1714).
  - Monthly earnings in South Africa 2017-2022 (Report 02-11-20), Table 1.4: median monthly earnings of domestic workers, R2,350 in 2022 (txt line 419).
  - QLFS Q4 2022 (P0211) and GDP Q4 2022 (P0441): context only. P0441 publishes no private-households value added (it sits in personal services).
