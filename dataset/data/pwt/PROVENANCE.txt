file: pwt110_ky_labsh.csv
contents: countrycode, year, cn, cgdpo, labsh, delta, pop_millions, pl_n, pl_gdpo for 2017 and 2022. cn is the capital stock at current PPPs (million 2021 US dollars). cgdpo is output-side real GDP at current PPPs in the same unit. labsh is the share of labour compensation in GDP. delta is the average depreciation rate. pop_millions is used only where WDI has no population row. pl_n is the price level of capital formation and pl_gdpo the price level of output-side GDP (both USA GDPo in the base year = 1). The capital-output ratio used in the build is (cn/cgdpo) times (pl_n/pl_gdpo): cn and cgdpo are both at current PPPs, so their ratio equals the nominal K/Y times PPP_Y/PPP_K; multiplying by pl_n/pl_gdpo removes that relative-price term and gives the nominal K/Y that matches the nominal-USD value added it is applied to. pl_n and pl_gdpo were extracted from the same pwt110.xlsx (10 small country-years have no price level and keep the PPP ratio).
years: PWT 11.0 runs from 1950 to 2023, so 2017 and 2022 are observed years, not extrapolations.

file: pwt_pim_capital.csv
generator: build/derive_pim_capital.jl (run `julia --project=earlier-build earlier-build/build/derive_pim_capital.jl` to regenerate).
contents: countrycode, year (2022), pim_gross_ky, pwt_gross_ky, logratio, replace. This is a derived cross-check, not an upstream download. pwt_gross_ky is the PWT 11.0 gross nominal capital-output ratio for 2022 ((cn/cgdpo) * (pl_n/pl_gdpo), the same quantity build/open_accounts.jl applies). pim_gross_ky is an independent perpetual-inventory estimate: starting from the 2017 PWT gross K/Y it rolls the ratio forward with the inflation/FX-neutral recursion (K/Y)_t = (1-delta)*(K/Y)_{t-1} + (I_t/Y_t), where delta is the PWT depreciation rate (interpolated between the 2017 and 2022 values) and I_t/Y_t is the nominal investment-to-GDP ratio from WDI gross fixed capital formation (sources/wdi/wdi_gfcf.csv, NE.GDI.FTOT.CD) over a geometrically interpolated nominal GDP path (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD endpoints). Where no investment series exists for a country (e.g. Nigeria: the World Bank publishes no NE.GDI.* for 2016-2022) the recursion holds the base-year K/Y constant (I_t/Y_t = delta, net investment zero), the conservative no-break assumption given flat real output. logratio = log(pwt_gross_ky / pim_gross_ky). replace = 1 where (i) |logratio| > 0.5 (above the 90th percentile 0.39 of the cross-country PWT-vs-PIM gap) AND (ii) pim_gross_ky is strictly closer than pwt_gross_ky to the cross-country median gross K/Y, so an implausible roll-forward (e.g. Yemen) never replaces a measured value. In the flagged cases open_accounts.jl uses pim_gross_ky instead of the PWT value (NGA 7.38->2.76, COD 0.61->3.44, SYR 0.34->0.89, TTO 13.1->3.20, VEN 23.2->8.86). The recursion is unit-free (a ratio of nominal flows to nominal GDP), so no currency conversion or deflator enters.
