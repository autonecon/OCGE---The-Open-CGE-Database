source: OECD Consumption Tax Trends 2024, Annex Tables 2.A.1, 2.A.2 and 2.A.3; European Commission Taxud.c.1(2021), VAT rates applied in the Member States of the European Union, situation at 1 January 2021; State Council of the People's Republic of China, Order No. 691, 19 November 2017, amending the VAT Provisional Regulations; State Taxation Administration announcement on deepening VAT reform (rates from 1 April 2019).
license: OECD data may be extracted and redistributed with attribution. The European Commission VAT-rate document is a public compilation supplied by the Member States. The Chinese instruments are public legislation.
accessed: 2026-10-18
file: vat_statutory.csv
contents: one row per country, year, and commodity. A commodity of * is the standard rate. A named commodity overrides it. Rates are percent of the basic-price sale.
use: final-demand product taxes (household, government, investment) are spread across commodities in proportion to these statutory rates times the basic-price purchase. The regional dollar total is held at the national-accounts wedge already in the master, so GDP, trade and the value-added floor do not move. The installed ad valorem rate is the statutory rate times that regional scale. The scale is written on the master as vat_scale_hh, vat_scale_gov and vat_scale_inv.
not_installed: the statutory level itself is not written onto the flows. On the 2017 master, replacing final-demand wedges with the unscaled statutory rates moves expenditure GDP by about 737 billion USD inside the OECD list, and the United States, which has no federal VAT, would lose a sales-tax wedge of about 570 billion and its GDP error against WDI would widen. Charging non-deductible VAT on the intermediate purchases of exempt activities, and removing it elsewhere, would raise the count of columns at the 0.0002 factor-bill token from 123 to 188 in 2017. That substitution is not in the master. Excise duties are specific in the statutes retrieved here and are not converted to ad valorem rates.
united_states: OECD Consumption Tax Trends 2024 states that the United States does not impose a VAT. No US rows are in the file. The existing national-accounts wedge stays uniform.
qatar_and_gulf: Qatar had no VAT in 2017 or 2022. A zero statutory schedule would not reallocate a positive wedge, so Qatar is not in the file. Other Gulf VAT laws were not transcribed in this round.
