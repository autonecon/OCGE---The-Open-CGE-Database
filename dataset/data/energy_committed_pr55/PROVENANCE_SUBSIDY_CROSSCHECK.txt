Cross-check of Gulf natural-gas subsidies. Not a price source.

File: gulf_gas_subsidy_crosscheck_2022.csv
Source workbook: IEA Fossil Fuel Subsidies Database, 2025 edition, "Subsidies
2010-2024.xlsx", updated 13/11/2025, CC BY 4.0. Sheet "Subsidies by country",
product Gas, column 2022. The unit printed on the sheet is "Real 2024 million
USD". The workbook has no domestic price column and no 2022 price. It is not
committed.

Nominal 2022 dollars: real-2024 value times the US GDP implicit price deflator
for 2022 divided by the deflator for 2024. Bureau of Economic Analysis, via
FRED series A191RD3A086NBEA, index 2017=100, annual, not seasonally adjusted,
downloaded 2026-10-20 from
https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=A191RD3A086NBEA.
2022 = 118.026. 2024 = 125.587. Factor = 118.026 / 125.587 = 0.9397947.

Consumption: Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy, 2025 edition,
sheet "Gas Consumption - Bcm", billion cubic metres, 2022. Bahrain is not a
separate row (it sits in Other Middle East), so no implied gap is computed.
The United Arab Emirates has an EI consumption row and no row in the IEA
subsidy file.

Implied gap, USD per MMBtu = nominal 2022 subsidy million USD times 1e6,
divided by consumption in MMBtu. The cubic-metre conversion is the EIA factor
in PROVENANCE.txt: 1 bcm = 1e9 * 35.3147 / 1e3 * 1.037 MMBtu.

Reference marker: the same EI workbook, sheet "Gas Prices", year 2022, Japan
Korea Marker (Platts JKM) = 33.978904 USD per million Btu. Footnote 2: S&P
Global Commodity Insights, copyright 2025. JKM is a delivered Northeast Asia
spot price, the closest cited marker on that sheet for Gulf LNG sales. It is
not a Gulf FOB netback. The IEA subsidy is a price-gap construct (subsidy
approximately (reference price − domestic price) times consumption). The
implied gap is subsidy divided by consumption. It is compared with
(JKM − official regulated price) only where a regulated price with document,
page and date has been entered. No Gulf regulated price met that test, so
reference_minus_regulated is blank and no ratio is computed. Nothing in
production_2022.csv was changed to match this table.

Oman and Bahrain have a zero 2022 gas subsidy on the IEA sheet. Oman's implied
gap is zero. That is the file, not a finding that the domestic price equals JKM.
