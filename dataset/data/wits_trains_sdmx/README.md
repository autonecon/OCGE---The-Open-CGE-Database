# Tunisia TRAINS 2016 MFN

**NOT FOR PUBLICATION.**

`tun_mfn_2016_reported.xml` is the WITS SDMX reported MFN for Tunisia (reporter 788, partner 000, product All, year 2016, datatype reported), refetched 2026-10-22 from the public API. The v3.x copy was not on this machine. Finance Law 2018 article 39 is applied in the S10 rebuild: lines whose 2016 simple average is 20 percent in chapters 25–97 become 30 percent. Annex rates are not applied.
